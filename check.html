More details: what the setup line does

Your browser normally stops pages opened from your own files from talking to programs on your laptop. The setup line tells Ollama to accept them. It's the setting that decides which pages can talk to your bot.

While it's set, any website open in your browser could also send requests to Ollama: use your models, or download or delete them. It can't reach your files. That's fine for today. To undo it afterwards:

Mac (it also resets when you restart your laptop): launchctl unsetenv OLLAMA_ORIGINS, then quit and reopen Ollama.

Windows: [Environment]::SetEnvironmentVariable("OLLAMA_ORIGINS", $null, "User"), then quit and reopen Ollama.

If you'd rather not change the setting, and you have Python, open the pages through a small local server instead: in this folder run python3 -m http.server 8000, then open localhost:8000/check.html. Ollama already trusts pages opened that way.
